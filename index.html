About

Split The Bill

Split The Bill is a live music event dedicated to celebrating and documenting Galway's local music scene.

Our focus is on supporting young local artists and giving emerging bands a stage, while giving young people the space to freely enjoy themselves without limits.

We do this while capturing every performance so the night lives on beyond the room it happened in. This site is an evolving archive. Every event adds another chapter.
